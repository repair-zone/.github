# The Repair Zone

We're a volunteer repair team that runs The Repair Zone at [Open Sauce](https://opensauce.com). We fix exhibitors' gear and attendees' projects, help people repair things themselves, lend tools, and hand out the tape, solder, and wire that keep an event running.

> The Repair Zone is an independent organization. We're not part of Open Sauce or iFixit, though we work closely with both.

## What we do at events

- **We fix it**: our team repairs exhibitor equipment and attendee projects.
- **You fix it**: assisted self-repair at our side table.
- **Tool checkout**: borrow the tool you need and bring it back.
- **Consumables**: tape, solder, wire, and raw materials.

## What we're building

This org holds the software that helps us run the Repair Zone. Our main project is an app for:

- **Tickets**: exhibitors/sponsors/creators request help and follow the status live.
- **Inventory and tool checkout**: what we own, where it is, and who has it.
- **Quick logging**: recording a fix in a few taps, even in the middle of a rush.
- **3D print queue**: tracking print jobs from request to pickup.
- **Event map**: finding booths and dispatching the nearest available team member.

It's designed to work offline, because event Wi-Fi and cell service can be fallible.
